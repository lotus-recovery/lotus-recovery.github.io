Lotus — Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026 · v1.1 — plain-English on purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Evergreen Studio Pty Ltd (ABN 74 700 261 276, ACN 700 261 276) (“we”, “us”, “our”), a company registered in Victoria, Australia, collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you use the Lotus mobile application (the “App”) and our website (the “Site”, together the “Service”).

Lotus is a self-help app that supports people who want to reduce or stop gambling. Because of what the App is for, some of the information you choose to put into it is deeply personal. We have designed Lotus so that we collect as little as possible, we never sell your information, and the most sensitive things you record stay between you and your own account.

1. The short version

  • We never connect to your bank. All money tracking in Lotus is manual — numbers you type in yourself. Lotus has no ability to see your accounts, balances or transactions, and never will by design.
  • We never sell your information and we do not show advertising or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your recovery data is yours. Check-ins, moods, journal entries, spend and savings logs are stored in your account, protected so that only your login can read them, and used only to show the App's features back to you.
  • Analytics are optional and anonymous in content. If (and only if) you switch on “share anonymous usage data”, we record which features are used — never amounts, never moods, never journal text.
  • You can delete everything from inside the App at any time.
  • Lotus is for adults (18+).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Email address and password. Used to create and sign in to your account. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Sign in with Apple. If you choose “Continue with Apple” instead of a password, Apple confirms your identity to us and gives us your name and an email address. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email” option, we only ever receive a private relay address and never your real one. We never receive your Apple password or anything else from your Apple account.

2.2 Profile and settings

  • Your country/region (used to show the correct local support services and currency — you choose it, we do not track your location).
  • Your estimated monthly gambling baseline (a number you enter during setup, used for the App's opportunity-cost calculations).
  • Your consent choices and when you made them (agreeing to the terms, confirming you are 18 or older, and the optional analytics and email-updates toggles). We keep a simple, append-only record of these consents so we can evidence them later.
  • A referral code (randomly generated for your account) and referral activity if you use the invite feature.

2.3 Recovery data you choose to record

These exist only if you enter them, and are the heart of what the App does for you:

  • Daily check-ins, including an optional mood.
  • Gambling spend logs (amount and date).
  • Savings logs (amount, date, and which goal they count toward).
  • Goals (name and target amount).
  • Journal entries and “reasons” (free-text notes you write).
  • Urges you logged as ridden out.
  • Progress through guided programs (which step of which program you are on).

We treat all of the above as sensitive information because, taken together, it relates to your health and wellbeing. We collect it only with your consent (you provide it by entering it), we use it solely to provide the App's features to you, and we never use it for advertising, profiling or any purpose you would not expect.

2.4 Feedback

  • If you send feedback from inside the App, we receive the message you write plus your platform (iOS/Android) and App version. Feedback is write-only from the App — it is stored so that only we can read it, and you can delete your own feedback by deleting your data.

2.5 Information stored only on your device

  • Small preferences like your chosen theme and dark-mode setting are stored on your device itself and are not sent to us.
  • Optional reminders (morning encouragement, evening check-in nudge) are off by default and, if you turn them on, are scheduled entirely by your phone. No reminder information is sent to us or anyone else, and their wording never mentions gambling or money.

2.6 Website waitlist

  • If you join the waitlist on our Site, the form provider (Formspree) passes your email address to us. We use it only to tell you about Lotus's availability, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

3. What we deliberately do not collect

  • No bank or financial account access — ever. Lotus does not connect to banks, cards or financial data aggregators. This is a permanent design decision, not a missing feature.
  • No precise location. Your region is a setting you pick, not something we detect.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or other device data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or marketing SDKs.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the App: storing your entries so your lotus, goals, insights and program progress work and stay in sync when you reinstall or change devices.
  • To operate the referral program (matching a redeemed code to the account that shared it).
  • To respond when you contact us or send feedback.
  • To send you occasional email updates about Lotus — only if you turned on the email-updates toggle (it is off by default). You can turn it off at any time in the App.
  • To understand feature usage in aggregate via optional analytics (see section 5).
  • To keep the Service secure (for example, rate-limiting abusive requests).

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that affects you, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

5. Analytics (optional, off unless you opt in)

During setup (and at any time in the App's settings) you can choose whether to “help improve Lotus with anonymous usage data”. If you leave it off, no analytics are sent. If you turn it on, we send a small set of named events to PostHog, our analytics provider, hosted in the European Union:

  • Events like: app opened, check-in completed, saving logged, spend logged, program step completed, referral redeemed, Plus trial started, lotus rebirthed, feedback submitted.
  • What is never sent: money amounts, journal or feedback text, moods, goal names, or anything you wrote. Events carry only the event name and basic context such as App version.
  • Events are linked to your account's internal user ID (a random string), not your email address.
  • Opens of the calm kit and crisis pathway are deliberately not tracked at all.

6. Where your information is stored and overseas disclosure

  • Main database: hosted with Supabase in Sydney, Australia.
  • Analytics (only if opted in): PostHog, hosted in the European Union. This means opt-in analytics events (described in section 5) are disclosed overseas to the EU.
  • Subscriptions: RevenueCat, a US-based subscription-management provider, records whether your account has an active Lotus Plus subscription. This means your account’s internal user ID and your subscription status are disclosed overseas to the US. RevenueCat never receives your name, email, payment details, or any of the recovery data described in section 2.3.
  • Waitlist form (Site only): Formspree, a US-based form provider, processes the email address you submit.

7. Who we share information with

Only these service providers, and only so the Service can function:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication (Sydney, Australia).
  • PostHog — optional analytics (EU), only if you opt in.
  • Formspree — website waitlist form (US), only if you use it.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — if you subscribe to Lotus Plus, the store processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (US). It sits between the app store and Lotus and tells us only whether your subscription is active, linked to your account’s internal user ID. If you sign in with Apple, Apple also acts as your identity provider (section 2.1).

Beyond that, we would disclose personal information only if required by law (for example, a valid court order), or to protect someone's life or safety where the law permits it. We will never voluntarily hand your recovery data to gambling companies, advertisers, or data brokers, and we do not respond to commercial requests for user data.

8. How long we keep information, and how to delete it

  • We keep your information while your account is active, so the App can keep working for you.
  • Delete from inside the App: Settings → delete my data permanently removes your check-ins, spend and savings logs, goals, journal entries, reasons, urge logs, program progress, feedback, lotus history and profile from our database immediately.
  • Account login record: deleting your data from inside the App also removes the email/login record itself, so nothing about you is left behind and you do not need to contact us. If deletion ever fails to complete in the App, contact us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions are separate: a Lotus Plus subscription is billed by the app store (Apple or Google), not by us, so deleting your data does not cancel it. You can cancel any time in your device’s app-store subscription settings, and the App reminds you of this before you delete.
  • Consent records (the fact that you agreed to the terms and confirmed your age) may be retained as a legal record even after deletion, as permitted by law.

9. How we protect your information

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database rules enforce that each account can only ever read and write its own rows (row-level security on every table).
  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Rate limits and database-level checks protect against abuse.
  • We never hold bank credentials or payment card details, because the App never collects them.

No system is perfectly secure, but if we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected users and the relevant regulator as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme (Australia) and equivalent laws elsewhere.

10. Your rights

10.1 Everyone

  • Access the information the App holds about you (most of it is visible in the App itself; contact us for a copy of the rest).
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your information (see section 8).
  • Withdraw consent for analytics or email updates at any time in the App's settings.

10.2 Australia

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. If you have a concern, contact us first and we will respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

10.3 United Kingdom and European Union

If UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies to you, our legal bases are: consent (for the recovery data you enter, analytics, and email updates) and legitimate interests (for security and service operation). You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority.

10.4 United States (including California)

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we have not done so. California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising those rights. Exercise them via the App or by contacting us.

10.5 Canada

We handle personal information consistent with PIPEDA. You may access and correct your information and withdraw consent as described above, and may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada with concerns.

10.6 New Zealand

We handle personal information consistent with the Privacy Act 2020 (NZ). You may access and correct your information, and may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

11. Children

Lotus is for people aged 18 and over. Account creation requires confirming you are 18 or older, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the App before the change takes effect. We will never reduce your rights over information already collected without your consent.
