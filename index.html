Inside the app

A daily check-in that can't be failed

One tap, any mood — steady, wobbly, or it's been hard. No streaks, no zero. A relapse muddies the water; the lotus keeps growing.

Money kept, made visible

A skipped bet becomes progress toward something real — flights, a licence, a buffer between you and the bad weeks.

Your bank stays yours

Lotus never connects to your bank — the connection doesn't exist. You track by hand; it sees only what you show it.

The calm kit and crisis pathway are free, forever

Safety will never be behind a paywall or interrupted by an upsell. This is a founding rule, not a pricing decision.
